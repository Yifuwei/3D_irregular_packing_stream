## Protocol and limits

Each run performs one full randomized CA plus 50 LS neighbor evaluations, with no wall-time cutoff, no restart and no early stagnation exit. A single-bin solution may stop early with an explicit reason, although none of the 29 completed cases used that exception. LS starts from its own CA solution and continues from the accepted layout. This is the original improved_GRASP, not the separate weight-radio or GRASP_ILS version.

Three-seed parameter selection is not independent validation of generalization. Equal work does not imply equal runtime. Geometry validation does not certify complete insertion trajectories. The archived 180-second experiment is not mixed into this batch.
